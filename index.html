From the Press Room

How the Mini works

  • The grid is CSS Grid holding 23 one-letter text inputs, each labelled with its row, its column and its clues.
  • The highlighting uses :has() and :focus to light up the word you are in and its clue.
  • The solved stamp is the browser’s own form validation: form:valid only matches once every letter is right.
