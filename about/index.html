Xuanye Zeng

Computer science graduate student at Northeastern University in Seattle, building backend and AI systems that check their own work.

Computer science is my second engineering degree. I’m a master’s student at Northeastern University’s Seattle campus, after a bachelor’s in mechanical engineering at the University of Science and Technology Beijing, where a research project on reinforcement learning for context management in AI systems pulled me toward code.

Most of my work sits where backend engineering meets AI. This summer at Smart Carrier (Onki) I built the service that turns messy merchant product data into the structured records a voice AI agent runs on. In my own projects I like to keep models honest: validate input at the door, keep deterministic rules in charge of decisions, and gate model output in CI.

This arcade is where I practice the front end. It opens with a crossword built from nothing but HTML and CSS, and it gains a new game every few weeks this semester.
