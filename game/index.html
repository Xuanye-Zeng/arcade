How to play

  • Click or tap any open square and type. The current word and its clues are highlighted.
  • Tab moves to the next square and Shift+Tab goes back. On a phone, use the arrows or the Next button above the keyboard.
  • Turn on Check answers to see which letters are wrong. They turn red and get a red corner.
  • Once every square is correct, a “Solved” stamp appears under the grid.
