How to play

  • Click or tap any open square and type. The word you are in and its clues light up.
  • Tab moves to the next square and Shift+Tab moves back. On a phone, use the arrows or Next key above the keyboard.
  • Turn on Check answers to mark wrong letters with a red corner.
  • Fill every square correctly and the puzzle stamps itself solved.
